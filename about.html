About Ewha

Ewha Womans University is a private women's research university in Seoul, South Korea.
It was originally founded as Ewha Haktang on May 31, 1886, by missionary Mary F. Scranton.

Today, it is one of the world's largest female educational institutes and one of the most prestigious universities in South Korea.
